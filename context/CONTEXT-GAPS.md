2026-07-28 | Why is `checkout`/`fork_at` on a single large text container still ~2x slower than 1.12? | `should_rebuild` in `RichtextDiffCalculator::calculate_diff` and `ListDiffCalculator::calculate_diff` (crates/loro-internal/src/diff_calc.rs) rebuilds the tracker from empty via `build_full_crdt_tracker` whenever `has_retreat` is set, i.e. on *any* backwards checkout, instead of only when the incremental tracker cannot express an op's context. 2026-07-28 | Which ways of relaxing the richtext/list `should_rebuild` have already been tried and ruled out? | Four attempts, all measured before #1058 landed, all reverted. The numbers below predate it, so re-baseline before trusting them; the conclusions about which relaxations are unsound do not depend on the base. (a) Gate the rebuild on a `tracker.all_vv()`-based "tracker already covers both versions" predicate: unsound, and the predicate is near-vacuous - `start_vv` is the LCA, so it is a subset of both sides by construction, and version vectors cannot see the real failure, where `all_vv()` reports full coverage but the tracker holds *wrongly positioned* content. (b) Drop the `has_retreat` clause: zero gain. Instrumenting `calculate_diff` on B4 shows the rollback fires with `has_retreat=true`, `lca!=from=true` and `src_not_in_ctx=true` simultaneously, and `lca == to != from` holds in *every* backwards checkout, so the other clauses keep the rebuild. (c) Disable the rebuild outright: `crates/fuzz` fails `diff_calc_text_lca_biased_regression` and `diff_calc_text_checkout_then_splice_position_regression`. Both are `Checkout` followed by further edits - the checkout's own diff is fine (a B4 oracle comparing against a doc imported only up to the target version matches exactly), the damage is that the leftover tracker mispositions *subsequent* ops. Any experiment that only checks the checkout output will wrongly conclude this is safe. (d) Defer the rebuild to the next `apply_change` via a `base_untrusted` flag: all 127 fuzz tests pass, but B4 gets *slower* - checkout 22.1ms -> 59.8ms, fork_at 69.8ms -> 174.6ms (median of 5), because `checkout -> attach -> fork_at` has an op to apply right after every checkout, so the deferred rebuild comes due immediately and happens more often than before. | Every one of these looks obviously correct on paper; only instrumentation and the fuzz corpus tell you otherwise, and (c) additionally needs a multi-step oracle rather than a single-checkout one. | context/diff-calc-replay-base.md
